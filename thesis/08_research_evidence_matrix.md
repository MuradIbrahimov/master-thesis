# Research hypotheses and evidence matrix — working draft

**Source status.** The five working hypotheses below come from the improved research plan supplied by the author. Their exact final wording remains subject to supervisor approval. The project workspace contains no standalone plan file or bibliography, so the author's supplied statements are the wording source for this draft. The evidence and assessments are mapped to executed Stages 01–07 without changing the analyses. Intervals are 95% unless stated otherwise. A positive paired loss difference *left minus right* favours the right model. Stage 05's original 2020–26 test and Stage 07's later exploratory reuse of those dates are kept separate.

**Interpretive rule.** Regime contrasts are retrospective, model-implied changes with delta-method uncertainty; forecast differences use matched dates and paired time-series resampling. In-sample AIC/BIC and smoothed state probabilities cannot answer a forecasting hypothesis. The revised GPR input lacks verified historical release-time availability, so all GPR-using forecasts are historical pseudo-out-of-sample exercises.
